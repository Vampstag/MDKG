Pink Roulette introduced Ciara Brosnan as its new face under Everyday Beauty Companion, positioning the brand as an accessible, playful part of everyday beauty routines.

Built around “Beauty Made Easy, Every Day,” the campaign explores beauty across the rhythm of a day, from getting ready and quick touch-ups to simple moments of self-care, with Ciara bringing the brand’s playful spirit to each one. The visual direction brings together campaign imagery, marketplace content, and social-first motion into one cohesive visual language.
