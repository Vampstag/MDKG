Asritektur is an Indonesian architecture and interior design studio, founded in 2024. Its output is practical: plans, working drawings and renders that carry a house from idea to site.

The studio wanted to be read as more premium and more elegant. That direction came from its marketing lead. As Content Lead, working remotely, I turn it into daily decisions about voice, visuals and content.

“Show the house as it is lived in, not only as it renders.”

Creative Approach

01

Working from the archive

The studio could not produce new content, so nothing could be shot. What it had was an archive: renders show how a house looks, drawings show why it works. The content is built from that, one room at a time, starting with a two-storey house in Jakarta.

02

Copy that names what a room does

A caption says an island is there for cooking together, or that the family room sits beside the stairs. General design talk could belong to any studio; a specific fact belongs to this house. Each claim is checked against the drawings first, because a render can place rooms side by side that the plan puts on different floors.

03

A reel that leads with feeling

The render reel opens with “POV: Pulang ke rumah yang rasanya seperti villa pribadi”, a line about coming home, before any plan or spec.

Same house, two ways of seeing it

Key Insights

Check captions against the drawings

A sentence about a room is only as strong as the drawing behind it. I check each claim against the plan before it goes into a caption.

Specifics beat design talk

An early line about details being “thought through twice” was cut: it could belong to any studio. A specific fact belongs to this house, and for me that is what premium means here.
