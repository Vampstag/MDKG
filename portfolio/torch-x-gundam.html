Torch's first collaboration with Mobile Suit Gundam, launched in February 2026, bringing the mecha franchise into Torch's visual language for its existing fan community. The collection spans backpacks, sling bags, t-shirts, caps, and pouches.

I handled cinematography, editing, and color grading across the key video deliverables, building an industrial, atmospheric look that stayed consistent across setups and formats.

Reimagining Gundam
for the fans who
grew up with it.

Creative Approach

A muted industrial grade with restrained contrast and subtle retro-sci-fi texture referenced the franchise without letting the IP overpower Torch's own design language.

Lighting changed, schedules were tight, and reshoots were limited.

So the grade adapted to each setup, keeping its character while holding one visual language.

Key Insights

Process Can Be Part of the Story

The strongest piece was Behind the Design at 317K views, not the main announcement. The audience wanted to see how the collaboration came together.

Authenticity Comes From the Details

Color, pacing, and atmosphere were enough to connect to the franchise without reproducing it literally.

Adaptation Is Part of the Craft

Different shoots produced different footage. Post-production made it feel like one body of work.
