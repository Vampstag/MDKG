**[Overview]**

Pink Roulette introduced Ciara Brosnan as its new face, under Everyday Beauty Companion. The idea was to position the brand as an accessible, playful part of everyday beauty routines.

The campaign is built around one line: Beauty Made Easy, Every Day. It follows beauty across the rhythm of a day, from getting ready and quick touch-ups to simple moments of self-care, with Ciara bringing the brand's playful spirit to each one. The visual direction brings campaign imagery, marketplace content, and social-first motion together into one visual language.

That's Pink Roulette.
