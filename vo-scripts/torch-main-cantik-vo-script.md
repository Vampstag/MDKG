# VO Script — Torch Main Cantik Case Study
Target length: under 60 seconds. Tone: direct, first person, matter-of-fact, no hype-reel delivery. Matches the page's "Listen to Project" narrator slot at `#overview` (audio file: `assets/audio/vo-main-cantik.mp3`).

---

Torch's Women Collection launched under the line Main Cantik, across three films that follow a woman living in the city. Teaser one, Woman to Woman, opens quiet, carrying the weight of her working day. Teaser two, Sibuk Itu Cantik, follows her through that same day with a more confident rhythm. And the launch film, Campaign Release, brings the collection into focus.

I edited and color graded all three in DaVinci Resolve, start to finish, in one week. The footage came in Rec.709, not Log, so every shot had to be corrected and matched on its own terms, scope by scope, rather than pulled from one flat base.

The grade follows her through the city: cooler light where the film admits how heavy her day gets, golden hour once she's taking it on with confidence, and a cleaner look for the launch that moves toward the collection's own sage and cream.

The product comes last on purpose. The campaign was about the woman first.

That's Main Cantik. Busy days, beautifully lived.
