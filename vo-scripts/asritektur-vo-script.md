# VO Script: Asritektur Case Study
Target length: ~25-35 seconds. Tone: direct, first person, matter-of-fact, no hype-reel delivery. Matches the page's "Listen to Project" narrator slot at `#overview` (audio file: `assets/audio/vo-asritektur.mp3`).

Only says what the page says.

---

**[Overview]**

Asritektur is an Indonesian architecture and interior design studio, founded in 2024. The studio wanted to be read as more premium and more elegant, and as Content Lead I turn that into daily decisions about voice, visuals and content.

**[Creative Approach]**

The studio could not produce new content, so the work starts from its archive of renders and drawings, one room at a time. Captions name what each room does, and every claim is checked against the plan. The reel opens with the feeling of coming home.

**[Key Insights]**

A sentence about a room is only as strong as the drawing behind it. And a specific fact is what makes it premium.

That's Asritektur.
