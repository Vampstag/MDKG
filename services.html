Project Terms

01

Timeline & Kickoff

The project timeline starts after the shoot day, or once all your materials and briefs are locked in.

02

Payment Terms

A 50% down payment is needed to start. The rest is cleared before final files are sent. Invoices have a 7-day window.

03

Deliverables

You'll get the final ready-to-post videos. Project files and raw footage aren't included unless added as an extra.

04

Revision Scope

Revisions cover tweaks to the agreed concept. Changing the whole idea midway will need a new quote. Extra revision rounds cost IDR 150K each (or IDR 200K for bigger projects).

05

Additional Requests

Need something extra outside our agreed plan? I'll let you know the cost before we do it.

06

Usage Rights

The videos are all yours to use once fully paid. I just keep the right to show them off in my portfolio.

07

Project Cancellation

Down payments can't be refunded once we start working. Any finished work up to the cancellation point will be billed fairly.
